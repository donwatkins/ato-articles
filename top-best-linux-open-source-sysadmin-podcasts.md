# The top Linux, open source, and sysadmin podcasts on my list

Learn, laugh, and code: The best podcasts for open source enthusiasts.

**Published:** February 11, 2025  
**Source:** [https://allthingsopen.org/articles/top-best-linux-open-source-sysadmin-podcasts](https://allthingsopen.org/articles/top-best-linux-open-source-sysadmin-podcasts)

I spend a lot of time on the road or walking around my village and enjoy listening to podcasts about various topics. One of my interests is keeping up with the latest news and information about Linux and open source. I discovered Jupiter Broadcasting, an open source podcasting network, that covers open source topics and is under a share-alike license. I met Jupiter’s cofounder <a href="https://www.jupiterbroadcasting.com/hosts/chris/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Chris Fisher</a> when I visited <a href="https://system76.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">System76</a> headquarters in 2018.

I am a regular listener to<a href="https://www.jupiterbroadcasting.com/show/linux-unplugged/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> Linux Unplugged</a>, billed as “Your Weekly Linux Talk Show.” Each week, Chris Fisher is joined by Brent Gervais and Wes Payne to discuss the latest developments in Linux and open source. I learn something new with every show, including great show notes with links to all topics covered in the shows. All the shows are released with Creative Commons share-alike licenses.

Jupiter Broadcasting has a family of shows, which includes <a href="https://coder.show/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Coder Radio,</a> which is a weekly show about software development. The network also produces another of my favorite podcasts, <a href="https://www.jupiterbroadcasting.com/show/self-hosted/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Self Hosted</a> which features hosts Chris Fisher and <a href="https://www.jupiterbroadcasting.com/hosts/alex/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Alex Kretzschmar</a> as they share their experience with self hosting and guide you through their latest adventures. In the show they chat about innovative software and cutting-edge hardware to optimize your network, manage smart devices, and ensure the security of your data on cloud services.

**Read more: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6kQg8bP1Ji5BbNF2iBUn_36uH3WCkRt8" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">We Love Open Source podcast interview series</a>**

<a href="https://latenightlinux.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Late Night Linux</a> is a family of shows started by producer <a href="https://joeress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Joe Ressington</a>. The Late Night Linux family includes Late Night Linux, <a href="https://2.5admins.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">2.5 Admins</a>, <a href="https://linuxafterdark.net/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Linux After Dark</a>, <a href="https://linuxmatters.sh/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Linux Matters</a>, <a href="https://www.linuxdevtime.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Linux Dev Time</a>, <a href="https://hybridcloudshow.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hybrid Cloud</a> and <a href="https://askthehosts.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Ask the Hosts</a>.

All the shows are great, and I started listening to Late Night Linux about five years ago. I look forward each week to engaged conversations and rollicking good times with host Joe Ressington, joined by Sysadmin <a href="https://fosstodon.org/@felimwhiteley" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Félim</a>, technical writer <a href="https://fosstodon.org/@degville" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Graham</a>, and engineering manager <a href="https://fosstodon.org/@8none1" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Will</a> as they delve into the latest developments and releases in the world of free and open source software. They also tackle broader issues and trends with a candid approach. Expect colorful language, strong opinions, and ribbing aimed at Félim over AI and the cloud.

2.5 Admins has become another favorite since it debuted. Each week features two sysadmins called <a href="http://www.allanjude.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Allan Jude</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/jim-salter-linux" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Jim Salter</a> and Joe Ressington, producer/editor who can just about configure a Samba share.

Linux After Dark is another favorite. On each show, Sysadmin <a href="https://www.podchaser.com/creators/chris-pearse-107to4n3xd" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Chris Pearse</a>, cloud consultant <a href="https://gary.thewilliams.ie/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Gary</a>, and developer/admin <a href="https://maydur.st/about/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">May</a>, join Joe to talk about their recent Linux-related experiences and discuss some of the more philosophical aspects of being a Linux user.

All the Late Night Linux family podcasts are released with Creative Commons share-alike licenses.

Immersing yourself in Linux and open source podcasts is a fantastic way to stay updated, learn from experts, and connect with a passionate community. Whether you’re a seasoned enthusiast or a curious newcomer, these podcasts offer invaluable insights, tips, and stories that can help you navigate the ever-evolving landscape of open source technology.
