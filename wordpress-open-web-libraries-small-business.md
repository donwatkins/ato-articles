How WordPress empowers libraries, communities, and creators worldwide.

WordPress remains one of the most accessible and customizable publishing tools in 2025—especially for community organizations, libraries, and small businesses. Built on a powerful open source foundation, WordPress helps users organize content intuitively through categories and tags. Categories define broad themes such as “Digital Literacy” or “Community Programming,” while tags like <a href="https://donwatkins.info/tag/python/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">#Python</a>, <a href="https://donwatkins.info/tag/golang/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">#GoLang</a>, or <a href="https://donwatkins.info/tag/javascript/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">#JavaScript</a> make content easier to find and connect.

## WordPress in our library system

In the <a href="https://cclsny.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Chautauqua-Cattaraugus Library System</a>, WordPress has become essential for digital outreach. Each member library—from small rural branches to urban centers—can maintain its own affordable, mobile-friendly site. These sites reflect local priorities, whether promoting youth programs, sharing health resources, or hosting community events. With responsive themes and accessibility plugins, WordPress ensures every visitor—on desktop or mobile—can easily explore what their local library offers.

## Beyond libraries: Powering culture and commerce

WordPress isn’t just for nonprofits. It powers millions of sites across industries—from cultural institutions and local shops to independent creators and grassroots movements. The <a href="https://wordpress.org/showcase/tag/culture/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">WordPress Showcase</a> features how communities worldwide use the platform to tell stories, sell products, and organize for impact.

## Easy installation for Linux users

Linux users can install WordPress on distributions such as Ubuntu, Fedora, or Debian with just a few commands. Prefer a faster setup? <a href="https://www.turnkeylinux.org/wordpress" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">TurnKey Linux</a> provides a ready-to-deploy WordPress virtual machine. Download the <a href="https://www.turnkeylinux.org/download?file=turnkey-wordpress-18.2-bookworm-amd64.iso" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ISO</a>, spin it up in your favorite virtualization environment, and you’re live. The full source code is available on <a href="https://github.com/turnkeylinux-apps/wordpress" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GitHub</a> for those who want to customize or contribute.

## Mobile publishing made simple

In a mobile-first world, WordPress keeps creators connected. Its <a href="https://wordpress.org/mobile/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">official apps</a> for iOS and Android make it easy to post updates, add photos, or respond to comments on the go. Whether you’re documenting a local event or updating your organization’s site from the field, publishing is just a tap away.

## Learn, host, and grow

New to WordPress? The <a href="https://wordpress.org/support/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">official documentation</a> offers tutorials, guides, and community tips for every level of experience. WordPress also recommends <a href="https://wordpress.org/hosting/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">hosting providers</a> that balance reliability, performance, and cost. For educators and beginners, platforms like <a href="https://wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">WordPress.com</a> and <a href="https://edublogs.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Edublogs</a> offer free hosting with built-in support—though with limited plugin options.

## Join the open source community

WordPress thrives on global collaboration. Developers, designers, educators, and advocates <a href="https://make.wordpress.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">contribute</a> daily to improving the platform. The core source code is open on <a href="https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GitHub</a>, and the community welcomes anyone who wants to help shape the future of open publishing.

Whether you’re creating a site for a library, launching a local business, or blogging your story, WordPress remains one of the most inclusive, flexible, and empowering platforms on the web. It’s more than a CMS—it’s a foundation for connection.
